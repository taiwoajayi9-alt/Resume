### About me

I've spent 8+ years taking products from messy problems to launch and scale across **fintech, payments, AI/data and workforce tech**, in Africa, Europe and global markets.

Most recently at [@moniepoint](https://github.com/moniepoint), I led merchant financing (inventory, asset and solar finance, and BNPL) for an ecosystem of **2M+ merchants**. Before that I was VP of Product & Commercial at **Propel**, built AI products at **Coresight Research** and **Centricity.ai**, set up product analytics at [@nordhealth](https://github.com/nordhealth), and started out in workforce transformation at **Doheney**.
